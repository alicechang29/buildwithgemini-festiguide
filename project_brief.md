# My agent: FestiGuide (Personalized Festival Schedule & Discovery Concierge)
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps music festival attendees curate their ideal schedule and discover new artists while balancing crowd navigation, weather conditions, and wellness breaks.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Favorite artists, music tastes/genres, dietary preferences, walking endurance, rest pace.
- Tools: Festival lineup lookup, stage distance/layout calculator, weather forecast, similarity/discovery matcher.
- Catalog/UI: Curated timeline cards, stage schedule tables, artist discovery cards with audio/bio snippets.
- Image gen: Personalized festival "dream lineup" poster or daily schedule graphic.
- Sandbox: Route & conflict optimization algorithm (balancing overlapping sets, transit time, and food/rest buffers).

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for constraint/schedule optimization, Google Maps routing API, Cloud Trace for latency tracking.
